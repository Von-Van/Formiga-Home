Formiga Home

Formiga Home lets you step inside the houses in Formiga Desktop's village. To install it, drag
Formiga Home into Applications. Formiga Desktop will find it there on its own, and open it
whenever you click one of the houses.

Formiga Home never changes your colony. It keeps only how each house is arranged inside, along
with a few keepsakes and journal notes of its own. If anything goes wrong during a visit, the
household simply goes back to the desktop as it was.
