# Changelog

This page records what changed in each version of Formiga Home. Each version's section doubles as
its release notes: `.github/workflows/release.yml` publishes it alongside the downloads when the
version is tagged.

## 0.67.11 (2026-10-16)

Home is built on Formiga Desktop 0.67.11 and still reads household version 11 and travel version 5,
so it works with the same visits as before.

- Using a saved design keeps what is shown on furniture a newer Home put in the room, instead of
  moving it off.

## 0.67.9 (2026-10-16)

Home is built on Formiga Desktop 0.67.9 and still reads household version 11 and travel version 5,
so it works with the same visits as before.

- What hangs on a far wall (frames, postcards, ribbons, Battle medals, photos) now lies along the
  wall, slanting as its top does, instead of sitting over it face-on. Frames have a slim moulding
  and only as much mount as fits, so frames side by side meet rather than overlap.
- Using a saved design in a room keeps any furniture a newer Home put there, and fits the design
  around it.
- If Home can't write the list of who is indoors (a busy file on Windows, say), it tries again
  instead of leaving Desktop showing the wrong companions outside.
- A project stage readied by a visitor's arrival or a sleepover now shows its "ready on the project
  board" notice, where before the arrival notice hid it. The arrival still goes in the journal.

## 0.67.8 (2026-10-15)

Home is built on Formiga Desktop 0.67.8 and reads household version 11, which offers the keepsakes
a colony won in Formiga Battle, and travel version 5. Designs are kept in Home's own data folder,
so nothing about them reaches Desktop.

- A Designs page in Arrange Mode. The whole house, or one room, can be saved to a gallery by name:
  its sizes, finishes and pieces, and where the colony's things are shown, but never the keepsakes
  themselves or anyone who lives there. A design is tried in the house first, with anything it
  cannot bring named and why, and used as one change that undo takes back.
- Share saves a design as a file to pass on, and a design file opened is tried in the house before
  it is used or kept. The finds tab is now called Finds, to make room for Designs.
- Formiga Battle's cup trophies and medals, for the Backyard, Woodland, Millpond and Sandy Cove
  Cups. A trophy is a little brass cup on its arena's own stand (a wooden plinth, a mossy stump, a
  millstone and driftwood), and a medal hangs on its cup's own ribbon. They can be set down, hung,
  carded or framed like any other find, and a keepsake this Home does not know yet still gets its
  card.

## 0.67.7 (2026-10-15)

Home is built on Formiga Desktop 0.67.7 and reads household version 10, which keeps a household's
projects with its home, and travel version 5. With an older Desktop, the project board says
Desktop needs updating, and nothing else changes.

- A project board, on its own page of the notebook while living in the house. Five projects to
  begin with: a window garden and a puppet theatre to build, cosier beds and a family table to
  improve stage by stage, and a hearth that grows with the household's history. The board lists
  each in a line, with where it stands; choosing one shows what its next stage asks for, how far
  along each thing is, and what finishing it gives.
- A project's stage comes from what happens at home anyway (snacks shared, naps, games, plants
  tended, finds looked over, friends come over and staying over), from the days the colony has
  lived and the keepsakes the house has, and from souvenirs anyone in the colony has brought home
  from Formiga Hill, which count alike for every house. What each house makes is its own. Nothing
  costs anything, runs down or waits on you.
- Finishing a stage, whenever you like, gives the house something for good: a piece the household
  made, set in the house where there is room; pieces already there made better where they stand,
  with whatever is on them; or a new floor or wall. Each stage finished goes in the journal, and
  the board keeps a list of everything made at home, with when.
- Twelve new pieces, all made by households: a window box and the window box in bloom, a puppet
  theatre and its Fairground curtains, quilted, feather and canopy beds, gingham and harvest
  tables, and a hearth, with a mantel for keepsakes and then a garland. Three new finishes come
  with them (hearthstone floors, trellis and gingham papers), and two more arrive with the days
  for every house: oak parquet and a sage wainscot.
- A small mark on the board's tab when something on it is new or ready, a quiet line when a
  stage becomes ready, and one when a new souvenir from Formiga Hill counts towards a project.
- The finds on shelves and tables and what residents wear are drawn in what they are made of, so
  an acorn is brown and a teacup china, each with a few tones of shade. They are drawn by Formiga
  Desktop, so this comes with Desktop 0.67.7.
- The notes page's ruled lines scroll with what is written on it. They were painted with the cover,
  so scrolling a long page slid its words across lines that stayed put; now each line stays under
  whatever it was under, worn in the same places as it goes.
- Each page of the notes keeps its own place: scrolling down the furniture, turning to the rooms
  and back finds the furniture where it was, and a short page is never opened part of the way down
  because a long one was.
- What is written on the notes sits on the page's ruled lines: each line of the journal, each
  resident's name with their portrait standing on the line, what the chosen one is doing, the
  houses next door and the hints at the foot. The notes are set in a larger type, body text at
  14 points and small print at 12, as Desktop's notebook is.
- Quieter words on the notes read as easily as the rest, darker by daylight and lighter after dark,
  and the mode tab not chosen is lettered more brightly on the leather.

## 0.67.6 (2026-10-12)

Home is built on Formiga Desktop 0.67.6 and reads household version 9 and travel version 5, so a
household in Desktop's Echo Fox Mode comes in as foxes. It opens the same houses as before.

- A household that comes in while Desktop's Echo Fox Mode is on is a skulk of foxes here too: each
  resident and friend Desktop draws as the Echo Fox is drawn as it in every room, doing everything
  it would do as itself. Its name, likings and home are its own, and it comes in as itself the next
  time the house opens after the mode is switched off.

## 0.67.5 (2026-10-08)

Home is built on Formiga Desktop 0.67.5 and reads household version 8, which lets it tell Desktop
who is in the house. It still reads travel version 4 and opens the same houses as before.

- A small card of faces in the corner of the house says who is home, one cell for each resident
  and each friend lent for the visit. Click a face to send them out to the desktop, where they
  live in the colony as usual while the house stays open, and click it again to have them in. A
  resident kept out stays out the next time the house opens. A friend is out on the desktop until
  it comes over and again once it has gone home. With an older Formiga Desktop, everyone is in and
  the card is not shown.

## 0.67.3 (2026-10-06)

Home's version now matches Formiga Desktop's, so the apps released together carry one number. Home
is built on Formiga Desktop 0.67.3 and still reads travel version 4 and household version 7, so it
opens the same houses as before.

- Residents no longer slide across the floor. After about 25 seconds in a house, every walk and
  other looping animation froze on one frame, so residents glided wherever they went. Formiga
  Desktop 0.67.3 fixes this in the drawing Home shares with it.
- Residents walk up onto a seat or a bed and back down off it, a step at a time, instead of
  jumping from the floor beside it and walking out through the furniture.
- A pat or a lift stops a resident's walk, so it answers the pat or dangles while carried rather
  than walking on the spot. Let go over open floor, it lands where it was let go.
- A resident patted while up on a sofa or bed stays up there to answer, and walks down afterwards.

## 0.1.1 (2026-10-06)

- Home is now built on Formiga Desktop 0.67.1, which keeps companions whole on every display. It
  still reads travel version 4 and household version 7, so it opens the same houses as before.
- The Windows installer has been run on Windows.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-06)

This is the first release of Formiga Home. Formiga Desktop 0.67.0 and later opens a house from its
village in Home, where the household that lives there goes indoors for a while, and comes back out
when the house closes.

- **The house.** Up to three rooms with doorways and a front door, seven floors, seven walls, and
  thirty-two pieces of furniture in three sets, drawn as one cutaway dollhouse.
- **The notebook.** The window is a leather notebook, the house on one page and its notes on the
  other.
- **The residents.** They live on their own, each by their own temperament, with a short list of
  things you can ask of each.
- **Arranging and finds.** Furniture moves and turns, and everything the colony has found can be
  shown, on its own card if it has no picture.
- **Company.** Visitors and favourites, photos, keepsakes and a journal, going next door, and
  friends who stay over.
- **Rehearsal.** Started on its own, Home opens Desktop's sample household or a house from a real
  save, read-only, and checks every layout by the same rules Desktop would.
